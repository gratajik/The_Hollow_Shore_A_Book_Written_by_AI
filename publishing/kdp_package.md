### Short Description (≈150 words)

Private investigator Maya Chen takes a fifty-thousand-dollar commission to investigate the apparent suicide of Sarah Blackwood on the family's private island off the coast of Maine. When she arrives, no one admits to hiring her. A storm cuts the island off. And the house, impossibly, is already familiar.

Within forty-eight hours Maya has confirmed Sarah was murdered, found hidden passages riddling the mansion, and begun recovering memory fragments from a childhood she swears she never lived. Someone has spent twenty-five years waiting for her to come back.

Atmospheric, unsettling, and propulsive, THE HOLLOW SHORE is a psychological thriller about suppressed trauma, medical manipulation, family loyalty as enablement, and the terrible gift of memory returning. For readers of Tana French, Gillian Flynn, and Kate Atkinson.

### Back Cover Blurb (≈225 words)

Five years ago, Maya Chen trusted her instincts and a woman died for it. Now her agency is six weeks from closing, and a commission letter has arrived on Eleanor Blackwood's stationery offering fifty thousand dollars to investigate the drowning of Eleanor's niece.

Maya takes the ferry to Blackwood Island as a nor'easter builds over the Atlantic. The Victorian mansion waits on the hill. The matriarch descends the staircase and says, coolly, that she never wrote the letter.

Someone did. Someone wanted Maya on this specific shore.

By the first night Maya has found Sarah's frantic journal pages. By the second she has traced her finger along wallpaper she has traced before. By the third she has pulled a sedated boy from an IV line in the basement, and by the fourth she understands that the island is not a stranger. It is a place her mind spent twenty-five years refusing to remember.

Sarah's death was not the beginning. It was a signal.

And whoever sent it is counting on Maya to finally hear it.

A psychological thriller about stolen memory, dynastic silence, and the investigators who become, in the end, the witnesses they were always meant to be.

## 2. Categories

**1. Books > Mystery, Thriller & Suspense > Thrillers > Psychological**
*Why:* Primary genre. Maya's memory recovery, the gaslighting-by-medical-authority spine, and the interior register (Tana French / Gillian Flynn) live here natively. *Competitiveness:* Very high, but also the highest-volume discovery category for this comp-stack. Non-negotiable.

**2. Books > Mystery, Thriller & Suspense > Mystery > Women Sleuths**
*Why:* Maya is a female PI protagonist, every chapter in her POV, solving a murder that becomes a conspiracy. Women Sleuths readers reliably cross-shop Flynn and French. *Competitiveness:* High but healthier than Psychological. Strong chart-permeability for mid-list launches.

**3. Books > Literature & Fiction > Genre Fiction > Family Life**
*Why:* The Blackwood dynastic-abuse core and Maya's reconciliation-with-parents arc make this a legitimate fit, and it gives the book a non-thriller discovery lane where French and Flynn also surface. *Competitiveness:* Moderate. Easier to chart, pulls literary readers.

## 7. Review Strategy

### ARC Distribution Plan

- **Target ARC count:** 75–100 readers. Goal: 30–40 verified reviews in the first 30 days.
- **Distribution channels:**
  - **NetGalley** co-op listing (via IngramSpark or a co-op like Victory Editing NetGalley Co-op). 60-day window starting 8 weeks pre-launch.
  - **BookSirens** for thriller-specific reviewers. 4–6 weeks pre-launch.
  - **StoryOrigin** for newsletter swaps with other psychological-thriller authors.
  - **Direct ARC team** built from existing newsletter (target: top 25 engaged subscribers).
- **ARC timing:** Send 8 weeks before launch. Reminder at 4 weeks. Final nudge 3 days after publication with direct Amazon review link.

### Blogger / BookTuber Outreach Targets

- **Book blogs:** The Nerd Daily, Novel Gossip, Crime by the Book, Bibliophile Book Club, The Real Book Spy.
- **BookTubers:** emmabooks, Steph Bohrer, Jen Campbell (crossover literary), Book Roast (for atmospheric picks).
- **Bookstagrammers:** search hashtags #thrillerbookstagram, #psychologicalthriller, #gothicbooks — target accounts with 5k–50k followers and active comment engagement (not vanity follower counts).
- **Podcasts:** *The Bestseller Experiment*, *Crime Writers On...*, *Novel Pairings* (literary-thriller crossover).

### Timing Recommendations

- **Launch month:** **September or October.** Psychological / Gothic thrillers peak in autumn; the book's October island setting is native marketing.
- **8 weeks out:** Cover reveal, ARC distribution begins, NetGalley listing live.
- **4 weeks out:** Goodreads giveaway (100 ebook copies), preorder campaign, first BookTok teasers.
- **Launch week:** $2.99 launch price, KU enrollment, coordinated newsletter + social blitz, ask ARC readers to post reviews on publication day.
- **Week 2–4:** Amazon Ads ramp (target comp-title ASINs: *In the Woods*, *Sharp Objects*, *The Turn of the Key*). Podcast appearances.
- **Day 30:** Evaluate review count, adjust price to $4.99 if momentum holds, plan month-two BookBub Featured Deal submission once the book has 50+ reviews at 4.2+ stars.
