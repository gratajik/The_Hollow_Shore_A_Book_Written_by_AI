### C1: Carved Wooden Bird Custody — Final Disposition Underdocumented

**Source row (from story.md Facts):**
> "Carved wooden bird | Given to child-Maya by Ethan. Unique Nova Scotia carving style. Maker's mark 'R' (Renault). | ch20 → forensics ch21"

**Chapter(s) affected:** ch22, ch23, ch24, ch30, ch31

**Evidence from prose:**

Ch22 (flashback): *"Ethan presses a small, smooth object into her palm. It is a bird, carved from driftwood."*

Ch23: *"She held up the small wooden bird."* (device being shown to forensics)

Ch24: *"Maya had logged it into the evidence box three weeks ago and then, two days back, signed it out again and slipped it into her pocket for reasons she had not examined."*

Ch30: *"Inside it were the personal effects the prosecutor had released once the chain-of-custody evidence had been duplicated. The half-finished bird from Silas's Nova Scotia workshop, still heavy in the unfinished wing, was wrapped in a sock. The original, the one Ethan had pressed into her hand at eight and that she had carried unknowing through a dozen apartments, is in federal evidence in Switzerland now."*

Ch31: *"On the shelf beneath the drawing, under its own small pane of glass, sat the carved wooden bird. It had gone from Ethan's twelve-year-old hand to her eight-year-old pocket, from her childhood jewelry box to a padded envelope her mother mailed north a year ago, from the padded envelope into a federal evidence box in Portland. The Bureau had released it in April with a chain-of-custody form three pages long. Maya had signed the last page and driven it up herself and given it to the Trust. Its maker's mark was turned up to the light."*

**Problem statement:**

The original bird's custody chain is now sufficiently specified across ch30–ch31: it appears in evidence from ch13–ch24, is released by the Bureau in April (per ch31), driven by Maya to the Trust, and shelved in the Cathedral's glass case. However, the prose introduces a SECOND bird (the half-finished one from Silas's workshop) in ch30 without narrative justification. The ch31 coda specifies only the original bird's fate; the reader must infer the half-bird was given to the Trust as well because it is physically present in ch31's epilogue, but no explicit handoff is narrated.

**Severity:** Major — the second bird is physically present in the epilogue display but lacks an on-page transfer scene or explicit statement of intent.

**Fix type:** surgical

**Suggested fix:**

In ch30, after the line *"The half-finished bird from Silas's Nova Scotia workshop, still heavy in the unfinished wing, was wrapped in a sock,"* add: *"She would drive it up in October, the week before the opening, and place it on the archive shelf beside the original. Margaret would frame it under glass. The two birds, one complete and one stopped mid-carving, would hold the story of Ethan's two hands—the boy's and the man's."* 

Alternatively, in ch31 before the epilogue coda, add a single sentence in the opening paragraph: *"The half-finished bird from Silas had arrived in the box with the original; both would be shelved together beneath the chalk drawing."*

Either addition closes the custody gap. Recommend the first (ch30 addition) because it honors Maya's agency in choosing to preserve both.
